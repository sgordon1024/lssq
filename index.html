Open design work for Last Saturday Side Quest

LSSQ, in progress.

Last Saturday Side Quest is a monthly weekend gathering for nomads, built on music, play, wellness and connection. Follow the work on a new logo, an easier-to-update website and event chat. Anyone can follow along or pitch in.

This isn't LSSQ's official site. For events, go to lastsaturdaysidequest.odoo.com.
