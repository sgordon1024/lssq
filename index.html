Open design work for Last Saturday Side Quest

A new logo, an easier site and event tools for LSSQ, made in the open.

Last Saturday Side Quest is a monthly weekend gathering for nomads, built on music, play, wellness and connection. This workshop is where its new logo, a website Jake can update himself, and event chat get designed and built. Anyone can follow along or pitch in.

This isn't LSSQ's official site. For events, go to lastsaturdaysidequest.odoo.com.

In review

Logo marks, round 1

Five sketches: four simple marks that work everywhere from a 16px browser tab up to a banner, plus a badge made for stickers and patches. Each one keeps the colors and the 80s feel of today's badge. Steve is reviewing them one by one.
