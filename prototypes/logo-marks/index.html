Last Saturday Side Quest · logo marks

One shape that still reads at 16 pixels.

Today's badge packs a turntable, a banner, the full name and eight confetti shapes into one image. It's fun at full size, but as an Instagram avatar or a WhatsApp group icon it turns into a smudge. Each option below keeps the colors and the 80s feel and gives LSSQ a simple mark that works from a browser tab up to a banner.

Option A

Record Q

The record from today's badge becomes the Q in LSSQ, and the tonearm, needle and all, becomes its tail.

This is the closest to what people already know, so it reads as a cleanup rather than a rebrand. Music is the pillar it puts first.

Option E · New

Record Sunset

A's record sets like a sun behind the mountains, under the banner from today's badge. It's made to be a sticker or a sew-on patch.

Thick outlines, a die-cut border, flat colors and big type, so it holds up printed or embroidered. It's for merch, so it pairs with A for avatars and the browser tab.

Option F · New

Stripe Letters

LSSQ written in one unbroken ribbon of three stripes, like a 70s racing stripe that never lifts off. Each stripe writes the whole word in turn.

There's no outline: where the Q crosses itself, the ribbon underneath is cut away, so it works in one ink too. The stripes stay parallel through every curve, so the same set works on its own as dividers, borders and corners. It's a wordmark, so A still covers avatars and the browser tab.

Picking one

These are sketches meant to set a direction. Pick a letter, or mix a few (for example, A for avatars, E for stickers and F as the wordmark, or A's record in C's four colors). The chosen mark then gets refined, plus a one-color version for stamps and screen prints, before it goes to Jake.
