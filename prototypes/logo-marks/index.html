Last Saturday Side Quest · logo marks

One shape that still reads at 16 pixels.

Today's badge packs a turntable, a banner, the full name and eight confetti shapes into one image. It's fun at full size, but as an Instagram avatar or a WhatsApp group icon it turns into a smudge. Each option below keeps the colors and the 80s feel and gives LSSQ a simple mark that works from a browser tab up to a banner.

Option A

Record Q

The record from today's badge becomes the Q in LSSQ, and the tonearm becomes its tail.

This is the closest to what people already know, so it reads as a cleanup rather than a rebrand. Music is the pillar it puts first.

Option C

Four Pillars

A compass star for a gathering that lands somewhere new every month. Each point is one pillar: music, play, wellness and connection.

It tells the whole LSSQ story in one shape, and each pillar's color can carry through to the site and event pages.

Picking one

These are first sketches meant to set a direction. Pick a letter, or mix two (for example, A's record in C's four colors). The chosen mark then gets refined, plus a one-color version for stamps and screen prints, before it goes to Jake.
